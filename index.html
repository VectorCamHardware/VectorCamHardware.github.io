VectorCam tools and build resources

VectorCam is a smartphone and a 3D-printed lens and light attachment that images mosquitoes in the field. This page collects the build guide and the working tools used to make, label and track devices.

Build a VectorCam

Everything needed to build a device: parts list, cost calculator, instructions and printable files. Open to anyone.

Field and lab tools

Web pages used by the team and partners to check devices, issue IDs, print specimen sheets and track equipment.

  • Public

    Image Quality Test →

    Checks a photo of a ChArUco board taken with a VectorCam and reports focus, exposure and white balance.

    /OpenVector/tools/image-calibration/
  • Team

    VCID Generator →

    Issues 17-character device IDs for VectorCams, VectorCatch traps and phones, and exports them for label printing.

    /VCID/
  • Team

    ID Printer →

    Prints specimen ID sheets and logs every printed range.

    /IDPrinter/
  • Access key required

    Device Tracker →

    Status and location of every VectorCam, trap and phone, keyed by device ID. For the hardware team only.
